We’re excited to share that Mario High Performance Diesel is an official Event Sponsor of Alberta Diesel Day 2026 — Western Canada’s biggest diesel motorsport event, returning to RAD Torque Raceway on June 27 & 28.

Whether you’re a diesel enthusiast, a competitor, or just looking for an incredible weekend of action-packed entertainment, this event has something for everyone. We’ll be on-site all weekend long and would love to connect with you in person.
